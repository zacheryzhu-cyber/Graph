Chiller Plant Graph Lab

What is this?

A fab's chilled-water plant, from cooling towers to the tools and cleanroom air, stored in three kinds of graph database.
RDF 1.1

The standard for shared, governed data models. A pipe with properties becomes its own node.

RDF 1.2

The new version. A pipe stays one statement, and you can attach statements about it, including claims nobody has confirmed.

LPG · Neo4j

A property graph built for traversal. A pipe is a relationship with properties.
