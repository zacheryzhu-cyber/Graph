UPW Graph Lab

What is this?

A small slice of a fab's ultrapure-water (UPW) plant and the cooling that serves it, stored in three kinds of graph database.
RDF 1.1

The standard for shared, governed data models. A pipe with properties has to become its own node (the "n-ary" pattern).

RDF 1.2

The new version. A pipe stays one statement, and you can attach statements about it: who documented it, how sure they are, even claims nobody has confirmed.

LPG · Neo4j

A property graph built for traversal. A pipe is a relationship with properties; paths and what-ifs are native.

Each step below is an everyday operator question. All three engines answer it, and each answer is checked against a plain JavaScript calculation over the same plant data (the reference). Where an engine differs, that difference is the lesson.
